**Bruce Barnett, "Awk" (the Grymoire tutorial)**,
<https://www.grymoire.com/Unix/Awk.html>. The page states:

> You are allowed to print copies of this tutorial for your personal use,
> and link to this page, but you are not allowed to make electronic copies,
> or redistribute this tutorial in any form without permission.

So no copy is kept in this repository. Tutorial 2 follows the page's table
of contents (basic structure, fields and the FS/OFS/NF/NR/RS/ORS variables,
associative arrays, printf, flow control, numeric and string functions,
getline, user-defined functions, patterns) with its own examples and
wording. Read the original in a browser next to it.
